Install in about a minute

Until the Google Play listing is live, the app installs straight from this site.

Download

On your Android phone, open dailyprophub.com/app and tap Download. If Chrome warns you, tap Download anyway.

Allow the install

Open the file. When Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for Chrome, then go back.

Install

Tap Install. If Play Protect says "unknown developer," choose More details, then Install anyway.

Turn on alerts

Open DailyPropHub and allow notifications for new picks and your favorite players.

The app tells you when an update is ready, and your favorites and settings carry over.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscriptions and no picks for sale. Tips through Venmo are optional.

When is it on Google Play?

Soon. Install it from this site now, and the download page will point to Google Play as soon as the listing is live.

Is installing from the site safe?

Android warns about every app that isn't from the Play Store. The app asks for no personal information. See the privacy policy.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. iPhone users can use the web dashboard in the meantime.

How often does it update?

Every day, before the first game. Live tracking updates while games are on.

Does it guarantee wins?

No. Model probabilities are estimates, not guarantees. DailyPropHub is for information and entertainment, and it doesn't take bets.
