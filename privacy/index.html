Privacy Policy

DailyPropHub app and dailyprophub.com · Effective October 30, 2026

The short version

DailyPropHub does not ask for, collect, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.

What stays on your phone

Your favorite players and teams, your parlay slip, and your notification setting are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What the app downloads

  • Pick data from dailyprophub.com (hosted on GitHub Pages). These are public files; the app sends no personal information to request them.
  • Live scores and player photos from ESPN's public sports feeds and image servers, and player photos from the NFL's public image servers.

As with any website or app that loads content over the internet, these hosts receive your device's IP address and basic request information when content is downloaded. That handling is covered by the hosts' own privacy policies (GitHub, ESPN, NFL). DailyPropHub does not receive or store it. All connections use HTTPS encryption.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app checks dailyprophub.com in the background and shows alerts on your device when new picks are published or a favorite player is featured. No notification service or device identifier is involved.

Sharing

When you tap Share on a pick or parlay, the app creates an image on your phone and hands it to your phone's share menu. Where it goes is up to you; DailyPropHub does not see it.

Age

DailyPropHub is intended for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children. It publishes statistical predictions for information and entertainment, does not accept wagers, and does not link to sportsbooks. If you or someone you know has a gambling problem, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
